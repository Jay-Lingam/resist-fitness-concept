Common questions

Do I need gym experience to join?

No. Most members start as beginners. Your first sessions are guided, on machines, at weights you can control.

How many days a week should I train?

Three days a week is enough to see progress when you are starting. Most people move to four or five once the habit is built.

How long before I see results?

You will usually feel stronger within two to four weeks. Visible changes typically take eight to twelve weeks of regular training and sensible eating.

What should I bring?

Clean training shoes, a water bottle and a small towel.
